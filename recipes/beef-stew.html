Beef Stew

Description

A stew made with slow-cooked meats, root vegetables, and a mix of earthy spices.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb beef, cubed
  • 2 onions, chopped
  • 3 carrots, chopped
  • 4 cups of beef broth
  • 2 tbsp of butter
  • 1tsp salt
  • 1 tsp dried sage
  • 1 tsp dried rosemary

Steps

  1. In a large pot, melt the butter. Add the onions and cook until softened.
  2. Add the meat and cook until browned on all sides.
  3. Stir in the vegetables and spices.
  4. Pour the beef broth, bring to a boil, the reduce the heat and let it simmer for 1-2 hours.
  5. Serve hot with a side of flatbread or rye bread.
