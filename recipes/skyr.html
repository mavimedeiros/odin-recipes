Skyr with Honey and Nuts

Description

Skyr is a traditional Icelandic dairy product, similar to yogurt. It is high in protein and can be enjoyed with a variety of toppings.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of skyr
  • 4 tbsp of honey
  • 1/2 cup mixed nuts, chopped
  • 1/2 cup mixed berries (optional)

Steps

  1. Divide the skyr among four serving bowls.
  2. Drizzle each bowl with honey and top with chopped nuts and berries, if desired.
  3. Serve immediately.
